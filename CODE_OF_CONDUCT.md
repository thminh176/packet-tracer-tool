# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Welcome contributors regardless of experience, identity or background. Critique code and claims with evidence; do not attack people, harass, discriminate, publish personal information or threaten anyone.

Respect the work and licenses of Cisco and independent developers. Do not request or share unauthorized courseware, private answers, bypasses or credentials. Discussions and contributions must stay focused on legitimate learning, inspection and troubleshooting.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, warn participants or restrict participation, proportionately and consistently. Report concerns privately through the security contact route in SECURITY.md; do not repost sensitive material in an issue. Maintainers must recuse themselves from reports involving their own conduct where another maintainer is available.
